Current Issues Summary 
1. Structural Instability

<START> and <END> appear in the middle of sequences

TIME_SIG changes randomly

NOTE_ON tokens often appear without corresponding DUR

2. Weak Musical Grammar

No explicit bar or position structure

No enforced NOTE_ON → DUR constraint

No transition control between token types

3. Representation Limitation

Linear token format (INST → NOTE → DUR) lacks temporal hierarchy

No bar-level organization

No position-level timing resolution

4. Generation Collapse

Instrument dominance over time (e.g., piano takes over)

Repetitive pitch and duration patterns

Loss of multi-track balance

Root Cause

The model is trained as a pure next-token language model,
but the token representation does not encode sufficient musical structure.
